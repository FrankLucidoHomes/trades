Professional pricing opinion

529 Lake Shore Road, Grosse Pointe Shores

Prepared for Toni Antonacci · November 14, 2026

My opinion: in my experience, a Lake Shore home with a view doesn't tend to follow price per square foot. Buyers are paying for the view, the address and how the price sits next to the other homes they can choose from. My sense is the better approach for 529 is to price it to bring more buyers in, rather than price it high and negotiate down.

The plan I'd suggest

  1. Price to attract. Position the home where it's likely to draw the most buyers in the first two weeks, when a listing tends to get its best attention.
  2. Sell the view first. Lead every photo set, video and headline with the lake, then the address, then the house.
  3. Show what's new. List every improvement since the August purchase so buyers can see how the home differs from the one offered last summer.
  4. Watch the first 14 days. Views, saves and showings are usually a good early read on whether the price is working. If they're strong, my preference would be to hold the price.
