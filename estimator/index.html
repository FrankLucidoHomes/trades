EQUAL HOUSING OPPORTUNITY

Taxes use 2025 total millage (summer + winter) from county and township records compiled by Transnation Title. Michigan taxable value uncaps the year after a sale, so the estimate uses 50% of the purchase price as taxable value, which is roughly what a new owner pays from the first full year. Homestead (PRE) rates exclude the 18-mill school operating tax. Special assessments, DDA levies and 2026 millage changes are not included; confirm with the city assessor. PMI, FHA MIP, VA funding fee, title and lender fees are estimates for planning, not a loan estimate. Seller customarily pays state and county transfer tax and the owner's title policy in Michigan, so those are left out of buyer costs.
